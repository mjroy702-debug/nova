What is Nova, exactly?+

Nova is a sample all-in-one workspace concept designed to help teams organize projects, coordinate work, and understand progress in one place.

Can I try it before paying?+

Absolutely. The sample pricing layout includes a free Starter plan and a 14-day trial concept for the Growth plan.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. The pricing section is a front-end demo. Connect it to your billing system to make plan changes work in production.

Will it work on my phone?+

Yes. This landing page adapts to mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop screen sizes.

Can I customize the design for my brand?+

Of course. Change the brand name, colors, content, pricing, and links in the HTML and CSS files.
